StuBook – Student Attendance App

StuBook is a student management application designed to maintain and manage students' records in one place. It helps manage student details, academic marks, attendance records, and other important information efficiently.

📌 Project Overview

StuBook is developed to simplify student record management by providing a centralized platform to store, access, and manage student information. It reduces manual paperwork and makes student data easier to organize.

✨ Features
Student Details Management – Store and manage student information.
Attendance Management – Record and track student attendance.
Marks Management – Maintain students' academic marks and performance records.
Student Records – Access and manage student information in one place.
Easy Data Management – Organize student details for convenient access.
User-Friendly Interface – Designed to make student record management simple.
🎯 Objectives
To maintain complete student records in a centralized system.
To simplify attendance tracking.
To manage academic marks efficiently.
To reduce paperwork and manual record-keeping.
To make student information easier to access and update.
